In every one, the rooms are real photos; the AI only ever lives in the moving blur between them. Watch each tile loop, then answer the two questions at the bottom.

1 · Hard cut

Hard cut (baseline)

No transition at all — before snaps to after. Punchy but abrupt; feels like a slideshow, not a film.

Fine, but leaves the "cinematic" on the table. This is what we're beating.
Spec default
2 · Match-dissolve

Match-dissolve

The old house melts into the new one on the same framing. Premium, calm, and the safest with the brain — no invented geometry at all. This is the "restrained dissolves" option from Q3.

✓ Lowest artifact risk · reads as reputation-grade. Used on most beats in the spec.
3 · Signature whip

Signature whip

A fast motion-blur swipe from one frame to the next — the "one flashy move." Energetic, scroll-forward. Use it once (spec) or on several cuts (Q3 "punchier"). The blur is the hallucinated part — it never slows enough to read as a real hallway.

△ Great once as a signature; overused it reads busy and raises artifact risk.
4 · Light-tunnel

Light-tunnel (bonus option)

Push through a bloom of light into the next frame. Dreamier, "transformation" feel. Not in the spec — showing it so you have the full menu.

△ Nice for a reveal beat; can feel gimmicky if repeated.
