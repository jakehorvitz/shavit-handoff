Writing + photography is the product

Listing as magazine feature: hero with title/price/tenure, four or five hero images, flowing prose about light and materials, floorplan/EPC/map links. No animation library at all. Its reputation comes from voice, not tech, which is exactly the "personal touch, not Claude" rule.

Quadplex 80 "Above the Clouds" · Outpost for Sotheby's · Awwwards SOTD + Dev 2025-05-15, CSSDA, FWA

The current ceiling of pacing (not of rendering)

Loader → "Enter experience / Enter without sound" → scroll-driven WebGL cloud fly-through; the tower tip breaks the cloud line only after ~40 scroll ticks; then floor plans, cross-sections, window vistas. Chris Loves Julia · "Where we started and now" · 2013, updated 2024

The voice register, not the design

First person, room by room, "here is what it looked like when we moved in, here is how it looks now", what was done. An owner talking, not a brand. This is the register the "from Jake, not Claude" rule wants for the chapter captions.

lowchrislovesjulia.com/our-house-where-we-started-and-now
Honest finding. No property-management company site in 2023–26 is award-tier; Belong (belonghome.com) is the most designed and it is a SaaS-style page. The closest award-tier work is branded rental buildings and design-led estate agents. Which means the bar for "best PM site in the Midwest" is low, and the bar for honest is what actually matters. Ruled out on register: volumetric clouds, 3D monoliths, terrain flythroughs, "sanctuary" copy, "Enter experience" loaders, apartment selectors implying inventory we do not enumerate.
