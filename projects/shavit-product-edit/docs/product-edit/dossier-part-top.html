Apple · AirPods Pro 3 · in-house · live Aug 2026

The canonical product edit, and the loading discipline

~32 viewports. Full-bleed hero loop (preload none, poster start/end frames), then chapters: a pinned VideoScrub where scroll drives currentTime, a ProductViewer whose short clips play only inside a scroll window, and everything else is opacity/transform staggers. Media is fetched only within ±100–150vh, unloaded after playing, and the whole effect switches off on low-capability clients.

What "product edit" means in 2024–26, from the winners: pin + swap (a full-bleed medium sticky for 2–4 viewports while short copy swaps over it) · scroll = time (video currentTime or a frame sequence; play-once-when-pinned is the cheap cousin) · zoom-in reveal (framed media scales to full-bleed while pinned; horizontal rails on vertical scroll) · type as image · a fixed backdrop with sections scrolling over it · and discipline about loading. GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis dominate the non-WebGL winners; Three only where 3D is the product. Nobody in the set used CSS scroll-driven animations yet (0 hits in computed styles across every browser-probed page).
