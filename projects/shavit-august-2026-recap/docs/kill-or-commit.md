# Stage 2 — kill or commit

Date: 2026-09-04  
Decision owner: Jake Horvitz  
Status: awaiting owner decision

## 1. Painful job and exact user

Jake needs to turn a scattered private brief—Shavit’s texts, HEIC/PNG attachments, prior Palmier edits, and current construction footage—into an August recap without mislabeling a property, flattening the story into a slideshow, inventing architecture, or wasting paid video-generation credits. The immediate user is Jake as editor/producer; the downstream reviewer is Shavit.

## 2. Existing tool that already does 80%

Palmier already handles the timeline, compositing, type, color, transitions, audio, and integrated video generation. The July Palmier project already supplies most of the visual grammar. The missing 20% is the high-risk reasoning layer: source-of-truth reconstruction, asset provenance, shot/copy decisions, safe camera blocking, and preflight approval before generation.

## 3. Jake’s unfair advantage

Jake has the full private client thread, original property photographs, documentary footage, Shavit’s exact copy and taste corrections, the July edit master, the existing August Palmier library, and a video-brain distilled from prior failures. A generic reel template has none of this context and cannot safely distinguish real property imagery from banned concept material.

## 4. One-day version

Commit one focused production-design slice:

- An annotatable HTML reel spec matching July’s measurable template.
- A 55–65 second shot-by-shot edit decision list.
- Shavit’s exact current six-project sequence: Norwood, Barry, Oak, Howder, Ludlam, and East Ewing.
- Thirty newly supplied images inventoried by project, with provenance-risk labels for retouched/PNG derivatives.
- Exact on-screen descriptions grounded in Shavit’s messages.
- Interactive local three.js previs for the proposed exterior rise/push and interior axis-push camera paths.
- A generation card for every proposed paid shot: source plate, exact motion-only prompt, current model candidate, duration, aspect ratio, risk, and approval state.
- A local acceptance command that checks the spec contract, runtime, asset references, privacy rules, and 3D tool loading.

Explicitly cut from the one-day slice:

- No Higgsfield/Palmier generation spend.
- No final motion generations, full Palmier assembly, mix, export, upload, or social publishing.
- No AI cleanup, outpainting, synthetic rooms, or synthetic architecture.
- No rebuilding unrelated held/blocked August reels.

## 5. Seven-day success metric

Within seven days of shipping the spec/previs:

1. Jake can approve or reject every shot and copy beat from the artifact with no ambiguity about the intended camera movement; and
2. the complete shot list reaches owner approval in at most one revision round; and
3. if generation is enabled and separately authorized, at least two selected I2V camera moves pass source-fidelity review within two attempts each, with zero invented property features.

## 6. Kill condition

Park the custom 3D/generation path and produce a conventional Palmier photo montage if any of these occurs:

- Jake or Shavit cannot approve a 55–65 second six-project story from the verified August material.
- The 3D previews do not make the planned motion materially clearer than annotated still frames.
- Two approved I2V candidates each fail source-fidelity review twice.
- The required generated-motion spend exceeds the owner-approved budget.

The fallback preserves the descriptions, July type system, and documentary assets while using only native keyframes and real footage.

## 7. Maintenance cost if it succeeds

Low. The spec and previs are static local files with no hosted service or database. Future monthly recaps require swapping the source manifest, copy, and shot cards; three.js/model or generation-price updates are occasional. Paid costs remain per-generation and must be approved each time.

## Recommendation

**COMMIT to the one-day spec/previs slice.** It answers the user’s immediate need—seeing the 3D camera ideas before Higgsfield—while containing spend and preserving a clean fallback to a conventional Palmier edit.
