Identical to shavitrootman.com's design system. Uniform colors were Shavit's explicit ask (5/22, 36:34). One accent does one job each.

ElementRule
TypefaceInter (Helvetica fallback), 800 uppercase for narrative type, tight tracking (-1%); tabular numerals for counters. Two weights max on any frame.
Type scaleHook/stake lines ≈ 92px @1080w · chapter lines ≈ 64px · tags/labels 28px letterspaced. Max 7 words on screen at once.
Safe zonesAll type inside 9:16 center-safe: 220px clear top (IG UI), 320px clear bottom (caption/CTA), 60px sides. 16:9/4:5 exports come from Higgsfield reframe + re-set type, not a blind center-crop (a 16:9 crop band is only y≈656–1263 — most type would fall outside it).
Vertical treatmentAll source photography is landscape. Three sanctioned treatments: (1) AI camera-move clip (§04) mounted on black with type in the dead zones — the default; (2) stacked split — before top / after bottom for dissolve beats; (3) real video footage reframed. No outpainting of property imagery — v3 wrote this rule and the shipped cut broke it (outpainted 9:16 plates are part of what read "a little AI" to Shavit). v4 enforces it: zero generated architecture, period. The plates916/ outpaints are retired from the film.
MotionPrimary language = AI camera moves on real photos (§04) — 4–8s per shot, one move per shot, dolly/push/lateral only, no whip or orbit on interiors. Ken Burns is seasoning, not the theme (Jake 7/3 revision): allowed on brief transitional/montage beats only, capped at ≤20% of still-derived runtime, and never on the hook, chapter opens, or the reward. Type layer keeps word-by-word rise-ins and ghost-dissolves for before→after. Type/graphics rendered in Remotion; Python/librosa builds the beat map; ffmpeg composites and encodes.
Word budgetLess type, more picture (Jake 7/3). Hard cap ≈30 words across the whole film: hook line · one setup + one payoff line per chapter · the closer. The story must read with the sound off AND the type off — before→after motion carries the narrative; type punctuates it. No frame holds more than one line at a time.
Human touchAt least 3 human/Midwest-texture beats from verified footage: Shavit or crew mid-rehab (the existing reel footage), porch/lawn/street warmth, flag or small-town detail for the July-4 angle. People make it a story about a community, not a slideshow about siding — his stated audience (tenants, neighbors, local lenders) sees themselves in it. Values register (Jake 7/3): Midwestern-American values, evergreen — work ethic, craft, stewardship, community, permanence. Every texture beat must express one of these; patriotic set-dressing (flags, holiday cues) is optional garnish, never the theme.
GradeBefore = cool, desaturated (-20 sat, cold shift). After = warm, lifted. Full warm saturation is earned — only from the Reward beat on.
AddressesNo full street addresses on screen, ever. Street names or project nicknames only ("SALEM STREET", "THE TRIO"). Protects tenants (occupied), the properties (vacant mid-rehab = theft vector), and third parties. Machine-checked via OCR at acceptance.

04 · The Higgsfield answer

v4: Higgsfield is the motion engine, not the polish layer

v3 rationed AI motion to ≤5 hero frames and filled the rest with Ken Burns — that ratio is exactly what Jake rejected ("bullshit Ken Burns… looks like a student made it"). v4 inverts it. The credibility rule is unchanged: AI may move the camera, never the house. Every pixel of property imagery originates from a real photo of the real property.

UseHow
YESCamera moves on EVERY still that appearsImage-to-video (Kling 3 class — best architecture stability; models_explore recommend confirms per-shot) with camera-motion-only prompts: slow dolly-in through the frame, lateral track, gentle push. One move per shot, 4–8s, slow = luxury. Every clip frame-diffed/eyeballed vs its source still — warped siding, bent window lines, or invented geometry = reject and re-roll. Stills that fail repeatedly run as stacked-split or inside real footage — never Ken Burns.
YESAI interior walkthroughs — 1–2 hero moments, rationedThe current RE-marketing trend (Kling-class walkthroughs) — Jake's ask 7/3, incorporated with the credibility rule intact: slow push along the photo's own axis into a living space (reward-house interior is the prime slot). The camera never turns a corner — passing beyond the photo's edge forces the model to invent rooms, which is exactly the "looks a little AI" Shavit flagged. Frame-diffed like every motion clip; invented geometry = reject.
CAND.Construction build-up effect — candidate upgrade, twice-gated (Jake's ask 7/3, demoted by council r2)Kling frames mode: start frame = a REAL before/mid-rehab photo (Salem Street has studs shots + the split-screen film), end frame = the REAL finished photo, same angle. Prompt (from the Market U tutorial, adapted): "A timelapse video capturing the entire construction process, featuring motion blur" — + dolly-in for interiors. The house visibly rebuilds itself INTO the true footage — generated pixels exist only in motion between two real anchors, so the credibility rule holds. ≤2 uses: Ch.1 studs→after, optionally the reward interior.
NOTutorial-exact fake construction statesThe source tutorial has Nano Banana invent a partially-built version of the house (or a night-scene variant). That renders a state that never existed on a real property — exactly the "looks a little AI" Shavit rejected. Only usable with Jake + Shavit's explicit ok; default dead.
YESUpscale to 2K/4KShavit's phone photos/older stills → upscale_image / upscale_video before motion-gen. Cleaner source = fewer i2v artifacts = fewer re-rolls.
YESSignature animationThe house-mark draw-on rendered as a clean motion asset (once, then static).
YESReframe assistsreframe to help derive 16:9/4:5 exports from the 9:16 master where the safe-zone crop needs help.
NOGenerated property imageryNo invented interiors, exteriors, or "enhanced" rooms that didn't exist. Misrepresentation risk on a reputation asset.
NOAI people / testimonialsThe bank-manager story is told in type, anonymous. No synthetic faces, ever.

  • ffprobe: runtime 52–62s · 1080×1920 · 30fps · H.264 yuv420p · +faststart moov before mdat · loudnorm: −14 LUFS integrated ±1 (check bounds exactly [−15,−13])
  • OCR pass over sampled frames: first narrative type ≤1.0s · no digit-leading street address anywhere · no wordmark before 50s · watermark present on sampled non-signature frames · every on-screen claim matches the approved claims list (FIFTY DOORS. THREE STATES. ONE STANDARD. / MICHIGAN / OHIO / INDIANA / MADE IN AMERICA, LOCAL TO THE MIDWEST / OWN. OPERATE. REPEAT. / MIDWEST GROWN. MIDWEST KEPT. / BUILDING COMMUNITIES, LOCALLY. / story lines verbatim) · ZERO "$" characters anywhere in the film (Shavit 7/3) · no two-letter state abbreviations (MI/OH/IN forbidden strings)
  • Ken-Burns budget test (revised per Jake 7/3 — capped, not banned): for every still-derived clip, estimate global motion as an affine warp of the source frame — near-zero residual = Ken Burns, parallax residual = true camera move. PASS iff Ken-Burns clips total ≤20% of still-derived runtime AND none lands on the hook, a chapter open, or the reward. Threshold calibrated on one known-good Kling clip + one deliberate Ken Burns clip before the loop starts.
  • Word-budget check: canonical line set v5 (Jake's slot A+B lines locked 7/4) — OCR unique narrative word count ≤ 50 (excl. watermark/signature) · no frame carries more than one narrative line · ≤7 words per frame
  • Silence-spike check: the Ch.2 drop-out hold is 0.8–1.2s in the beat map · desat-freeze frame present on the drop (visual event for muted viewers)
  • Plate hygiene (pre-composite): OCR every plate BEFORE compositing — FAIL on any IG chrome, browser/desktop text, or baked caption text (incl. "CLEVELAND, OH"-style lower thirds in reused reel segments — crop above the caption band) · captions.md and all deliverable text files pass the same zero-$/no-abbrev grep
  • Human review integrity: checks/visual_review.md must carry a human-signed reviewer line and postdate the master — a builder-generated attestation file is an automatic FAIL · every AI-motion clip has a diff image vs its source still in checks/motion_diffs/
  • Duplicate-plate check: perceptual-hash every composited plate — no photo appears in two different beats (Shavit's dupe catch, 7/3)
  • No-pulse (periodicity) test: FFT of the frame-diff signal over the signature segment — monotonic push-in passes, any oscillating component fails. (Raw frame-diff would false-fail the sanctioned 4% push-in.)
  • Brand-hex check on pre-composite layers: the Remotion type/graphics layer PNGs contain only the four brand hexes (±antialias tolerance) — checked before ffmpeg compositing, where dissolves/chroma make it unverifiable
  • Beat alignment: the edit's cut-list timestamps (incl. dissolve midpoints) verified against an independently recomputed librosa beat map, ±80ms
  • Same-house loop check: hook frame and reward frame are the same property — different photographs (different shot/crop/time-of-day); the hook/reward pair is exempt from the duplicate-plate phash collision at the stated threshold, everything else is not
  • Provenance: every composited asset has a manifest.csv row (source + rights basis); every AI-motion clip has a reviewed diff vs its source still; zero un-cropped screenshot frames in the pipeline
