# Instagram playbook — Shavit Rootman

**Job of the account:** make lenders, partners, sellers, and tenants who look Shavit up
conclude "serious operator, real work, local." Not follower growth. Every choice below
serves that read.

## Captions
- **First 125 characters carry the post** — that's what shows before "...more". Lead
  with the strongest concrete image or tension ("The bank wrote this one off." /
  "Every window on this street-side was boarded when we got the keys.").
- One idea per caption. Story arc: condition found → what the crew did → what it is
  now → quiet proof line. No dollar figures ever; the proof is the work itself.
- End with a low-pressure CTA matched to reputation: "Save this for your next walk-
  through", "DM us about the process", "Share with someone rebuilding in Michigan".
  Never follow-bait, never giveaways, never "tag 3 friends".
- Voice: plainspoken operator. Short sentences. No hype adjectives ("stunning",
  "unbelievable"), no emoji walls (0–2 max), no hashtag spam in the caption body.

## Hashtags
- 5–10, niche + geo, in the first comment or end of caption: neighborhood/city tags
  (spelled-out state), #rehab #renovation #realestateinvesting-type craft tags.
  Skip mega-tags (#realestate) — wrong audience, zero standing value.

## Carousels
- Slide 1 = the hook image + one stake line (uppercase Inter, brand tokens).
  Slides 2–5 = process in order. Second-to-last = the after. Last = proof line + mark.
- Alt text on every slide (accessibility + SEO).

## Reels/video captions
- The video templates own the visual system (templates/*.md + the Register's
  15-second chapter format). The caption's job: name the property nickname, the
  street name (no number), spelled-out state, and one process detail the video
  can't show.

## Cadence & profile
- 2–3 posts/week hard ceiling (ship enforces it). Pinned brand film anchors the
  grid; new work slots around it. Post when the audience is professional-awake
  (weekday mornings), not engagement-peak — lenders don't scroll at midnight.

## Never
- Dollar figures, house numbers, MI/OH/IN abbreviations, Zillow imagery, generated
  architecture, street/community disparagement, engagement bait, virality hooks.
