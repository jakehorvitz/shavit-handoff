# Facebook playbook — Shavit Rootman

**Audience here skews local:** neighbors, community groups, sellers with houses in the
service area, local tradespeople. Facebook rewards narrative + shares inside local
groups; that's the channel's job in this system.

## Posts
- Longer narrative works: 150–300 words telling one property's story start to finish.
  Open with the human beat ("This one sat empty through four Michigan winters."),
  not a sales line.
- **Praise the street.** On Facebook especially, the neighbors ARE the audience —
  frame every story as an investment in the block ("the street deserved better than
  a boarded-up corner lot"). The house can be the villain; the community never is.
- Photos: 3–5 real photographs per post (before/during/after order). No portal
  imagery, no generated architecture, people de-identified.
- CTA: "Know a house like this near you? Send it our way." — seller-sourcing framed
  as neighborhood care.

## Sharing mechanics
- Write so a local group admin could reshare it verbatim without it reading as an ad.
- Tag the city page (never a street address); spell out the state.

## Cadence
- Counts toward the same 2–3/week ceiling across all platforms + video. When IG and
  Facebook run the same property, stagger by a day and lead with different beats.
