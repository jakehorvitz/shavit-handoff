# LinkedIn playbook — Shavit Rootman

**This is the lender/partner channel.** Every post is read as due diligence by someone
deciding whether Shavit is a safe counterparty. Write for that reader.

## Posts
- Operator register: process, systems, craft, numbers-free discipline. The story of
  HOW the work gets done (sequencing trades, materials choices, what "rebuilt to
  code" actually took) beats any before/after glamour shot here.
- **First line is the hook** — LinkedIn truncates hard. One concrete operational
  tension: "The inspector failed the first framing pass. Good."
- Formatting: 1–2 sentence paragraphs, white space between every one. No hashtag
  walls (0–3 niche tags max). No emoji beyond an occasional single one.
- Proof line stands in for financials: MADE IN AMERICA · LOCAL TO THE MIDWEST.
  Never dollar figures — a lender who wants numbers should DM/call, and that's
  the CTA: "If you lend or partner in Michigan, Ohio, or Indiana rehabs, my line
  is open."

## What not to do
- No engagement-bait polls, no "agree?" one-liners, no broetry punchlines.
- No client/tenant identification. No addresses. Anonymize every story.

## Cadence
- 1×/week is plenty here; counts toward the global ceiling. Repurpose the week's
  strongest property story through the operator lens (content-repurposer flow).
