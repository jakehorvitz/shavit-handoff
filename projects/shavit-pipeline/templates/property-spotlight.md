# Template: property spotlight (single-beat)
platforms: ig, fb, li
format: single image/short carousel + caption

## Structure
1. ONE DETAIL — a single craft choice or turning point on one property
   ("the staircase the first contractor said to demo — restored instead").
2. WHY IT MATTERED — one paragraph, operator voice; process over glamour.
3. WHERE — street name or project nickname + city + spelled-out state, cited:
   on [F:prop.street]{<street>} in <city>, [F:prop.state]{<State>}.
4. PROOF LINE + platform CTA (playbook/<platform>.md).

## When to use
- Weeks without a finished before/after arc; keeps cadence inside the ceiling
  without manufacturing a fake milestone.
- LinkedIn: lead with the operational decision, not the visual.

## Rules hard-wired
- Every numeral inside a [F:id]{span}; unknown facts → shavit.sh fact add.
- No $ / no house numbers / no MI-OH-IN abbreviations / real photos only.
