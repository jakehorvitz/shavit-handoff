# Template: before → process → after
platforms: ig, fb
format: carousel (ig) / photo-narrative (fb)

## Structure
1. HOOK — the found condition, house-as-villain (never street): one stake line,
   uppercase, brand tokens. "[F:prop.street]{<street name>} had been written off."
2. CONDITION — 1–2 beats on what made it hard (cite anything countable:
   [F:prop.beds]{...}, timeline facts, crew facts).
3. PROCESS — the craft: what got rebuilt, in trade order. This is the reputation
   payload; give it the most slides/words.
4. AFTER — one wide, warm, real photograph. No superlatives; let it sit.
5. PROOF — "MADE IN AMERICA · LOCAL TO THE MIDWEST" + CTA per platform playbook.

## Rules hard-wired
- Every numeral inside a [F:id]{span}. Street name only, state spelled out.
- No $ anywhere. Real photographs only. De-identify people.
