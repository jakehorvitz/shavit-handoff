East Victoria Street Surprises , carousel spec

A numbered, swipeable photo carousel about East Victoria Street, Indiana: real surprises on the property when the CPM team took over. “Surprise” is used deliberately in place of the earlier “things wrong” framing: a surprise does not have to be a problem, it can be any unexpected real detail, which is a wider and more honest net to work from than a defects list. This is the companion still-photo post to the separate portfolio montage reel built the same day, and is built from the same standing rule as every other piece of Shavit content: every caption traces to either something plainly visible in a real photo, or the one fact already confirmed by a dictated meeting transcript. All nineteen real photos from this property's Drive folder have been downloaded and individually viewed for this spec. Four support a fully written, confirmed caption: a damaged exterior window, a bent fence panel, an octagonal accent window on the gable end, and the basement's prior use. A fifth candidate, a closer photo of the same basement window, was cut per Jake's note for reading as redundant with the exterior window item once both sat in the same short carousel. That is four confirmed items, not a padded seven or any other round number chosen in advance. The hook slide states the number honestly as four. The carousel closes on two separate beats rather than one, a plain proof-of-work photo followed by a text-only title card, split apart per Jake's note.
