"There's two reasons I'm being asked quite often — Shavit, how do you meet budgets? How does your company meet budgets?… The first one is thanks to Home Depot's program. It gives the investor audience the thing they actually want — proof he knows why his numbers work — and it does it structurally rather than numerically. Nothing else shot this year reads as operator-to-operator the way this does.
