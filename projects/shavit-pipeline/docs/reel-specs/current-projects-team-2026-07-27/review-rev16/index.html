The four Oak shots on their own

The block, frame by frame

Dissolves rather than cuts, deliberately. These are four rooms of one house. A hard cut between them reads as four separate properties; a dissolve reads as moving through a building. It is the same call as Barry's studs dissolving into the finished room. Three 10-frame dissolves, so the block is 4×44 minus 30 frames of overlap = 4.87s.
Why these are pre-rendered instead of keyframed in Palmier. The editor can only push into pixels the conformed clip already contains, and these are horizontal pans. A 3:4 photo cropped to 9:16 throws away 25% of its width, and the pan spends exactly that discarded strip — so nothing is ever upscaled past 1:1. A Palmier keyframe would have had to fake the pan with a zoom.
