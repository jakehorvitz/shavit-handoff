The motion rubric , what rev 6 has to clear before it is a reel

Rev 4 and rev 5 are not badly written. They are slideshows. Every property beat is a frozen photograph with a two to four percent zoom on it, which is invisible at reel speed, and the client's own standing register caps that treatment at twenty percent of runtime. This document is the pass or fail standard rev 6 gets built against: ten hard gates that block the ship, then forty scored criteria across motion, structure, cutting, type, finish, audio and delivery. Each one names how it is measured, not how it feels.

00How this rubric is graded

Three classes. Nothing is graded on vibes, every line names its instrument.

ClassEffectRule
Gate binary Ten of them. A single failed gate means the file does not ship, regardless of score. Most are inherited client law, not my preference.
Scored weighted Forty criteria across seven dimensions summing to one hundred. Each scores zero, half or full. Ship threshold is 82, and no single dimension may fall below sixty percent of its own weight, which stops a beautiful cut with broken audio from sliding through on total points.
Advisory noted Recorded in the verdict, never blocks. Audience read and taste calls live here, because a cut can pass every machine gate and still be wrong.

The one rule about the rules

A contact sheet plus a written attestation is not a machine check. Any criterion whose instrument says OCR, FFT, residual or ffprobe gets marked pass only after the tool actually ran and printed a number into reel/qa6/verdict.txt. This is the meta lesson the last build loop wrote down after marking machine criteria green off eyeballing frames, and it is the easiest way for this rubric to become theatre.

01The ten hard gates

Binary. Any single failure and rev 6 does not go to Shavit, let alone to feed.

G1

Pan on still treatment stays at or under twenty percent of still derived runtime, and never lands on the opening card, a block open, or the team reveal.

Instrument fit an affine warp of the source frame to each still derived clip's global motion. Near zero residual means the pixels only translated and scaled, that is Ken Burns. Parallax residual proves a true camera move. Sum the Ken Burns seconds against the budget. Threshold calibrated first on one known good generated clip and one deliberate Ken Burns clip. Script: checks/motion_residual.py.

Client law, kenburns-motion-cap, standing, as of 2026-07-03. Rev 4 and rev 5 both fail this outright.

G2

Zero generated architecture. Every pixel depicting a real property originates from a real photograph of that property. The camera may move, the house may not change.

Instrument every generated clip gets frames extracted at 0, 15, 30, 45 and 60, read side by side against the source still. Any object that appears, vanishes, relocates or changes shape kills the take. No outpaint, no uncrop, no enhance on property imagery.

Client law, no-generated-architecture, standing. Shavit detected synthetic pixels himself and rejected a promoted cut over it: "It looks a little AI." This gate is why the crane ups are capped at two seconds.

G3

No house numbers on screen. Streets are named by street name or project nickname only.

Instrument OCR over frames sampled at 2 fps. Fail on any token sequence matching a leading number followed by street words or a St, Street, Ave or Rd suffix.

Client law, no-house-numbers, standing, council derived. Occupied rentals have tenants and vacant rehabs are a theft vector.

G4

State names spelled in full where they appear. The standalone tokens MI, OH and IN never appear.

Instrument OCR forbidden string list over sampled frames. Also verify each state label matches the property actually on screen, because a prior cut reused one Michigan photo as the Ohio tile.

Client law, full-state-names, standing. Blocked input: the state per property is still unconfirmed, so rev 6 ships street names only unless you send them.

G5

No dollar figures, no return percentages, no deal specific asks anywhere on screen or in the caption.

Instrument OCR for the character $ and for percent tokens adjacent to return or yield words, plus a read of the caption copy.

Client law, no-dollar-figures. Compounding reason: return claims in public video create Regulation D solicitation exposure if a raise is live, per reg-d-solicitation-flags.

G6

Generated graphic layers stay inside the brand palette. Black #000000, white #FFFFFF, brass #B08D57 on rules and underscores only, gold #FFC000 on the mark plus at most one stake line. All set type uppercase Inter.

Instrument sample the pre composite RGBA layer PNGs, not the encoded video, because hex verification is impossible after H.264 chroma subsampling. Fail if more than two percent of visible pixels sit more than forty RGB distance from the four brand colors. Photography frames are exempt.

Client law, brand-tokens, standing. Note rev 4 shipped gold on caption body text, which is brass and gold role leakage.

G7

The ending overlays the final frame. No cut to a black logo card, and the mark itself never pulses, scales or rotates independently.

Instrument sample the last 2.2 seconds. Mean brightness must clear the black field threshold. FFT of the frame difference signal over the segment must show no oscillating component above 0.5 Hz carrying more than fifty five percent of spectral energy. A single monotonic push in of four to five percent is explicitly sanctioned and must not false fail.

Client law, static-signature and no-pulsing-logo. Retention reason on record: a long tail plus a black logo card kills looped viewing.

G8

Every named face is confirmed by Shavit in writing before render, and release status for all eight team slots is stated in the manifest rather than assumed.

Instrument a signed off name to face table in reel/qa6/roster-confirmed.md, quoting Shavit. Unstated release status is recorded as a caveat in the verdict, never silently passed.

de-identify-people standing, plus likeness-consent-releases: consent to be photographed is not consent to publish commercially. The current mapping was recovered from transfer identifiers in Shavit's roster message, and sorting the obvious way produced a completely wrong mapping. A wrong name on a real person's face is the worst defect available in this cut.

G9

If any photorealistic generated motion ships in the file, the post carries Meta's AI label before it is shared. new

Instrument a line in the delivery note stating which shots are generated, and the label applied at post time in the composer.

meta-ai-disclosure-mandate: Meta requires labeling photorealistic video that has been digitally generated or altered, and force labels what it later detects. AI edited real footage reads cleaner on platform than full generation, which is exactly what we are doing, but the disclosure is not optional and evasion is what the penalty language targets. New gate, because rev 4 and rev 5 had no generated motion in them.

G10

No photograph appears in two beats, and no placeholder string survives into the master.

Instrument perceptual hash every composited plate and compare pairwise. Separately, OCR the full frame set for bracket characters and the strings PENDING, TBD and TK.

Shavit personally caught a duplicate plate on a prior cut. The placeholder half is not hypothetical: rev 4 rendered "FRAMING THE NEW [ ROOM - PENDING ]" and "[ CAPTION - PENDING ]" in gold, on screen, in the master. That alone would have ended the review.

02Dimension 1, motion  ·  32 points

The heaviest dimension, because it is the entire reason rev 6 exists. The distinction that runs through all of it: a zoom scales pixels, a camera move changes the relationship between near and far objects. Only the second reads as cinema, and only the second survives the affine residual test in G1.

M1

At least seventy percent of property runtime carries true parallax motion. 6 pts

Instrument per clip affine residual from G1, summed as a share of property runtime. Full marks at 70 percent or above, half at 50 to 69, zero below 50.

This is the number that converts "make it look professional" into something a script can check.

M2

Every address block opens on an establishing move that travels, not on a static plate. 3 pts

Instrument first clip of each of the three blocks must classify as a true move, and must be the longest shot in its block.

Standard property grammar is wide to establish, then medium, then detail, and the wides are the shots held longest. Cutting's shot scale finding is the empirical version: short shots pair with tight framings, wides get held.

M3

Exactly one camera behavior per shot. No stacked dolly plus orbit, no move that reverses mid clip. 3 pts

Instrument prompt audit before generation, then motion vector sign check across the clip. A sign flip in the dominant vector is a stacked behavior.

Preflight 1.2, one-move-per-shot. Two stacked behaviors invite warping, and warping is what makes a generated clip look generated.

M4

Hero moves are large enough to read. Minimum eight percent apparent displacement across the clip, or a measurable parallax separation between foreground and background. 4 pts

Instrument optical flow magnitude at the frame corners, first frame against last.

This is the specific defect in rev 4 and rev 5. The spec called for two to four percent pushes, a defensible number for a long form film on a large screen and invisible on a phone in a feed. Either the move is a real move or the frame should be locked off on purpose.

M5

Motion energy escalates across the three blocks. Block three carries more movement per second than block one. 2 pts

Instrument mean optical flow magnitude per block, must be monotonically increasing.

M6

No generated clip contributes more than 2.0 seconds to the master, trimmed from the head. 4 pts

Instrument clip duration table in the cutlist, cross checked against the source generation length.

Measured on this exact material last night: a five second dolly in on the Norwood framing still held clean for about two seconds, then the black contractor bag and the broom dissolved, the insulation bag relocated onto the stud wall, and the framing layout changed. Verified stable at 0, 1, 2 and 2.5 seconds. The working recipe is generate five, keep the first two, throw the rest away.

M7

No move turns a corner, passes through a doorway, or travels beyond the source photograph's edge. 3 pts

Instrument prompt audit plus edge inspection of the final frame of every generated clip for invented geometry at the borders.

Preflight 1.3 and walkthrough-no-corners. These are the three intents that force the model to invent, which lands straight on gate G2.

M8

No generative motion on any human face, ever. Team portraits get deterministic transforms only. 3 pts

Instrument the generation ledger. Any job whose reference image contains an identifiable face is rejected at preflight, not at review.

Preflight 2.7 plus likeness-consent-releases. Identity drift on a real crew member is unfixable in post and is a consent problem, not an aesthetics problem. These are Shavit's actual employees.

M9

Every generated clip is conformed to 30 fps before it enters the timeline. 2 pts

Instrument ffprobe each intermediate. Fail on any 24 fps asset reaching the cutlist.

Higgsfield reframe outputs come back at 24 fps against a 30 fps delivery standard. This bit the vertical remaster already.

M10

No two consecutive shots move in the same direction. 2 pts

Instrument dominant motion vector per clip, compared pairwise along the cutlist.

Uniform motion direction across every beat is the named slop signature. Alternating the vector is also what makes a hard cut feel deliberate rather than accidental.

03Dimension 2, structure and story  ·  16 points
S1

Three address blocks, each running establishing move, then two to four interior beats, then a hard cut out. No address is revisited. 3 pts

Instrument the cutlist structure.

Your call this session. Rev 4 failed as a collage because it jumped address on every single cut, so nothing accumulated. Blocks fix that without collapsing to one house, because each block is internally continuous.

S2

The opening states what is being built right now, in seven words or fewer, legible on frame one, with no audio required. 3 pts

Instrument OCR frame one, word count, and a mute read of the first three seconds sampled at 8 fps.

Roughly eighty five percent of short form is watched without sound, and half of all drop off happens in the first three seconds. Your ask this session was that the intro be simple, and simple survives mute.

S3

Each block change lands on a texture or action match, not an arbitrary jump. 2 pts

Instrument written attestation naming the matched element at each of the two block boundaries, plus the frame pair.

Cutting on action hides the edit because eyes involuntarily track motion, which also masks the grade mismatch between two different houses. Rev 3 already had the right idea: Norwood ends on torn plaster, Barry opens on torn tile.

S4

One turn card hands off from work to people, on a hard cut, holding two to three seconds. 2 pts

Instrument cutlist plus OCR.

S5

Every team member gets an individual beat of equal length, within 0.1 seconds of every other. 3 pts

Instrument per slot duration table, standard deviation at or under 0.1 seconds.

Your words this session: "each individual person, we give them a little second." Equal length is the respect signal. Unequal beats read as a ranking of the crew.

S6

Three seconds or less of tail after the last new information. 1 pt

Instrument timestamp of last new on screen element against total runtime.

Council finding on the long form build: fifteen seconds of tail plus a six second logo card killed looped viewing.

S7

Total runtime between 40 and 48 seconds. 2 pts

Instrument ffprobe duration.

Long enough for three blocks plus eight faces to breathe, short enough to loop. The team block alone needs twelve seconds.

04Dimension 3, cutting and rhythm  ·  14 points
C1

Shot lengths cluster, they do not metronome. Coefficient of variation of shot length at or above 0.35, and no four consecutive shots within ten percent of each other. 3 pts

Instrument compute over the cutlist, excluding cards and the outro.

Across one hundred fifty films from 1935 to 2005, shot length sequences increasingly approximate a 1/f power spectrum, the same fluctuation pattern as human attention. Editors converged on runs of short shots and runs of long shots, not uniform pacing. Rev 4 runs eleven beats at 1.8 to 2.2 seconds, which is a metronome.

C2

Hold the wides, cut the details. The establishing shot is the longest in its block, detail shots the shortest. 2 pts

Instrument per block duration ordering.

C3

Cuts inside a block land mid move, not after the move has settled. 2 pts

Instrument optical flow magnitude in the final four frames of each clip must be above twenty percent of that clip's peak.

An action begun in shot A and completed in shot B reads as one continuous event, and cutting mid motion gives the most invisible joint. This directly contradicts the rev 3 instruction that every push "comes to rest before the cut", which is what makes the current cut feel like a series of separate photographs rather than one film.

C4

Every cut lands within eighty milliseconds of a beat, except cuts listed in the exempt array. 3 pts

Instrument recompute the beat map with librosa from the delivered master's own audio, not from the source track, then compare against build/cutlist6.json.

Verifying against your own beat map was rejected by council as unverifiable. Note also that cutting on every downbeat is what makes an edit predictable, so the exempt array is a feature: at least two cuts should deliberately land on a subdivision instead.

C5

One engineered silence before the turn card, and the card lands inside the silence rather than at its onset. 2 pts

Instrument true digital silence verified at or below minus 87 dB for at least 0.8 seconds, with the card timestamp falling at least 0.4 seconds after silence begins.

Cutting the music abruptly is the loudest interrupt available, and it is the only interrupt class that suits a reputation brand. Zoom punches and meme sound effects are the alternative and they read as hype.

C6

No five second window in which nothing changes: not the frame, not the type, not the motion, not the audio. 2 pts

Instrument sliding window over frame difference plus the type schedule.

C7

No snap transitions. Consecutive frame luma difference stays under thirty except at cuts declared hard in the cutlist. 2 pts

Instrument per frame luma delta scan. Properly crossfaded boundaries sit around fourteen.

This check caught real snap back artifacts in the long form remaster.

05Dimension 4, type and legibility  ·  12 points
T1

Safe zones respected: 220 px clear at top, 320 px clear at bottom, 60 px at the sides. 2 pts

Instrument bounding box of every composited type layer against the safe rect.

T2

Seven words maximum on screen at once, and every line clears a 240 millisecond per word reading budget within its own hold. 2 pts

Instrument word count per type block against that block's duration.

A ten word line inside a short beat is a reading speed traffic jam. The rev 4 turn card is four lines, at the ceiling before a statement reads as a paragraph.

T3

Street tags clear three percent of frame height in cap height. 1 pt

Instrument measure cap height on a sampled tag frame.

address-tag-scale, a one time cut note from your own remaster feedback: the originals sat near 1.5 percent and you scaled them roughly 2.3 times. Letterspaced micro labels are the first casualty of feed compression.

T4

Two type weights maximum per frame, on the locked scale: cards 92 to 132 px, captions 64 to 70 px, tags 28 px letterspaced, all uppercase Inter. 2 pts

Instrument the render code's font table, plus a visual read of one frame per type class.

T5

The bottom scrim is a smooth gradient from zero at y 900 to full at y 1400. No hard seam. 1 pt

Instrument column luma profile down the frame, looking for a discontinuity.

The long form build shipped a visible scrim seam at y 1150 to 1170 and it was a blocker.

T6

First narrative type is on screen within 1.0 second. 1 pt

Instrument OCR the first thirty frames.

T7

Type animates. Alpha reveal plus a staggered rise per line, cubic ease out, subpixel positioned. 2 pts

Instrument frame difference confined to the type bounding box across the first eight frames of each card must be non zero.

Pure PIL cannot deliver real kinetics without help. Council's ruling was that PIL is acceptable only with cubic ease out and four times supersampled subpixel positioning, otherwise the type layer needs Remotion. Frozen type on a moving plate is the tell that separates a template from an edit.

T8

Captions describe the room and the material, with no adjectives and no emotional claims. 1 pt

Instrument adjective scan of the on screen string list, reviewed against the register.

Shavit enforced this hard and repeatedly across the Howder review: facts, not emotion. One deliberate override sits in the cut, "So thankful for my team", which is him speaking in first person about his own people. Advisory, needs his yes.

06Dimension 5, image and finish  ·  10 points
F1

No source under 1080 px on its short edge receives a camera move. Soft sources are 2x upscaled before generation, or they stay locked off. 3 pts

Instrument source dimension table in the manifest, joined against the motion column.

Preflight 2.2, upscale-before-motion: motion on screenshot grade crops invents geometry. Live problem: six of the eleven current frames came out of Drive at 768 by 1024 or smaller, including all four Howder frames and the only Howder exterior. Soft as stills, dangerous as motion sources.

F2

Grade is continuous across every cut, and each generated clip is grade matched to the still it came from. 2 pts

Instrument mean RGB at a 54 by 96 downsample, compared across each boundary pair.

F3

No move reveals a frame edge. Plates render on an oversized canvas. 2 pts

Instrument scan the outer eight pixel border of every frame for black.

F4

Film grain finish applied uniformly across generated and photographic material. 1 pt

Instrument high frequency energy comparison between a generated clip and an adjacent still derived clip. A generated clip visibly cleaner than the photographs reads as AI.

The cheapest single thing that makes generated and real material sit inside one film.

F5

Expected warp zones are flagged and inspected: mirrors, glass, windows, railings, tile and any repeating texture inside a moving frame. 2 pts

Instrument a written zone list per generated shot, then a targeted read of those regions in the extracted frames.

Preflight 2.5. Relevant here because the Barry chimney frame is stone pattern tile and the Howder rafter frame is a repeating joist run, the two textures most likely to tile under motion.

07Dimension 6, audio  ·  9 points
A1

Integrated loudness minus 14 LUFS, plus or minus 1.5, true peak at or below minus 1 dBTP. 3 pts

Instrument ffmpeg -af loudnorm=print_format=json, parse input_i.

House standard, and the shipped long form master measured minus 14.25. No platform publishes an official Reels target, so every number circulating for Instagram is a vendor estimate. Mastering to minus 10 to hit harder on phone speakers costs the dynamic range that makes the C5 silence work.

A2

AAC 192 kbps at 48 kHz. 1 pt

Instrument ffprobe stream inspection.

A3

The bed is rights clean for a permanent commercial post, confirmed in writing, not assumed. 3 pts

Instrument a provenance row for the audio file in assets/manifest.csv naming the license basis.

Live risk. The current candidate is wildflower.mp3 and its rights basis is not recorded anywhere I can find. On the last build a licensed commercial track was flagged as risky baked into a permanent film, the flag was overridden, and the track died at Shavit review anyway. A baked bed must be royalty free or generated.

A4

Music enters on a declared frame, with a 0.5 second fade in and a 1.5 second fade out, and the chosen window starts on a downbeat. 2 pts

Instrument librosa energy and structure analysis to select the window, then verify the entry timestamp against the cutlist.

D1

1080 by 1920, 30 fps, H.264 yuv420p, faststart with moov before mdat. 2 pts

Instrument ffprobe, plus scan the first 4 MB of the file for atom order.

D2

Healthy bitrate. A 42 second vertical master lands between 8 and 12 MB. 1 pt

Instrument file size against duration. A 4 MB 57 second render was flagged as starved on a prior build, so low is a defect, not an efficiency.

D3

Poster frame is a real image with mean brightness above 28, not the black card. 2 pts

Instrument mean luma of the exported poster.

A mostly black cover fails for the same reason a black card ending does. The poster should be the strongest single frame in the film, which on current material is the Howder rafters against sky.

D4

No black frames anywhere outside the declared cards. 1 pt

Instrument mean frame luma below 10 outside the card windows is a fail.

D5

The 9:16 master is never blind center cropped to another aspect. Any 16:9 or 4:5 version gets its type re set. 1 pt
