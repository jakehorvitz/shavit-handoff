Portfolio Montage , reel spec

A ~15.5 second, fast-cut reel built entirely from existing still photos already sitting in Google Drive. No new footage, no host on camera, no stated opinion, ambient instrumental music bed only. The goal is breadth, not depth: four active projects across two states shown back to back in one short clip, so a viewer can see at a glance how much real work is happening right now. Every text overlay below states a fact, never an opinion, and follows the standing brand rules: no dollar figures, full state names, street names only with no house numbers, no contractions, and no em dashes.
