One pipeline gap worth knowing about

The facts registry does not exist

shavit-pipeline/facts/ is absent, so read_facts() returns empty and any [F:...] claim span in a content/<post-id>/post.md would fail lint. Seeding it is TTY-only by design: ./shavit.sh fact add, with Jake typing the values. Nothing in this session wrote to it, and nothing should.

Consequence: caption copy for Part 2 cannot go through the lint, verify, coach, review flow until the registry holds at least the 701e 4th St address and the auction figures. That is a five-minute TTY session, and it is the correct next step before any caption is treated as send-ready.
