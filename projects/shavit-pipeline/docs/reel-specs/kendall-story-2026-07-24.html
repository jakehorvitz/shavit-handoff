Provenance caution on the cover, raised 2026-07-24

Jake asked whether the finished exterior is actually this house or a compilation. Straight answer: every frame in both Kendall specs came out of the one Drive folder Shavit named Kendall, under Indiana, Past Projects. Nothing is pulled from another property. But the folder holds at least three visibly different exteriors: a grey house in snow with a large enclosed glass porch, a blue grey house in summer with mature hedges, and this white house with a black roof. They share a stone retaining wall and a similar ranch footprint, and a full renovation does change siding, roof, railings and planting. It is plausible they are one house at three stages. It is not proven, and a folder is not evidence.

Do not ship this cover until Shavit confirms that the white house with the black roof is the same building as the grey house with the enclosed porch, and that both are Kendall. One question, no cost. The interior frames in this spec are internally consistent and are not affected.

What is pending, and why it is only two things

The photography carries eight of the ten slides on its own. Two slides need Shavit, and they are the two that made East Victoria work: how the deal came to us and who was in the house before. Everything else is built.

Also confirm: the street suffix, since the folder name does not carry one, and that the city is South Bend. Neither is invented anywhere below; the on-screen copy currently says Kendall and Indiana only.
