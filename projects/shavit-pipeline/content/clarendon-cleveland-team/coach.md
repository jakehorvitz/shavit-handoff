# Coach — The Cleveland team (Instagram, GATED)

## Strategy

Shavit asked for "the Cleveland team" of the story of 3220 Clarendon. That is a
different post from the deal story, and a better one for the account if it can be
built, because it continues the series he launched himself last week with Lewis Lawn
Care: local businesses and crews that make the work possible. Series beat series
one-offs, and he wrote the format's copy himself, so the voice is already settled.

It cannot be built today. Not because of a missing decision, but because of missing
facts. There is no photography of this property or its crew anywhere: the Drive folder
is empty, the message thread has none, and local disk has none. A team feature with no
team photographs is not a team feature.

**Do not solve this with substitutes.** The temptation on a post like this is to fill
the frames with portal imagery, stock crews, or generated people. All three are
forbidden, and the reason is the whole point of the account: a lender who spots one
fake frame stops believing the real ones.

**Sequencing.** Ship the deal story first. It is buildable now, it establishes
Clarendon Avenue with the audience, and it makes the team feature land harder later
because the audience will already know what the crew built. If Shavit sends crew
photos this week, the team feature becomes the natural follow-up inside the cadence
ceiling rather than a competing post.

## rewrite: the ask to Shavit

The thing blocking this post is a request, so the request is what needs drafting. The
current ask in the thread is broad enough that Shavit answered it with a question.

Weaker (what was asked):
> Do you have any team footage you can send me

Stronger, because it names the deliverable and the permission in one message:
> For the Cleveland team post I need three things: a few photos of the crew on
> Clarendon, their names and roles, and your OK to publish each name the way you
> OK'd Lane. Also worth knowing if they are on Instagram or Facebook-only, since
> that changed the tagging on the Lewis post.

## rewrite: the closing beat

The Lewis post ended with "Who should we feature next?", which worked and should
repeat, but the thank-you should name the specific work rather than being generic
appreciation, or the series flattens into the same post every time.

Weaker:
> Thank you for everything you do.

Stronger:
> Three units, gutted and rebuilt. That was this crew.

## Note on naming people

Shavit's written go for Lane Lewis was explicit and specific to Lane. It does not
transfer. Each additional person needs its own confirmation before the name goes on a
slide, and until then the standing rule anonymises them.
