## What changed since rev 1, and why this is a rebuild

Rev 1 (8/20 13:30) was a type-only, eight-slide deck built on Shavit's first memo:
the seller's phone call, the sale that fell through, one unit already vacant. At
21:08 that night he sent a second case-study docx ("3220 Clarendon; match the
details you need from the metrics you used for 1919 Kendall") and at 21:15 to 21:31
about forty-five photographs. The docx supersedes memo 1 outright: a vacant
triplex on Clarendon **Road** in **Cleveland Heights**, Ohio, near University
Circle, acquired late 2025, comprehensively renovated, leased to tenants including
medical residents from outside the area, refinanced and kept. There is no phone
call in it. So the phone-call hook, the seller, and the "one unit was already
empty" beat are gone, not trimmed.

The photographs change the format: this is now a Kendall-system photo carousel
(bone band, SF headline, DM Sans subline, brass rule, index chip, SHAVIT ROOTMAN
mark) with a real frame on every slide.

## Strategy against the playbook

**The account's job** is the lender / partner / seller read: serious operator,
real work, local. Slides 03 to 07 are his BEFORE & AFTER section headers
(Kitchens, Bathrooms, Living areas, Common areas, Basement & mechanical systems)
each over his own paragraph for that room, which is exactly the "process is the
reputation payload" slide type. Slide 09, the hold, is his lesson "Refinancing
rather than selling" over his REPEAT sentence, the beat that separates Clarendon
from Kendall.

**The numbers stay on the website.** Every figure in the docx is barred by the
no-dollar rule. The trimmed L36 ("Following renovation, the three units were
leased.") shows how: his sentence, the figures removed.

**The befores are in the deck, by Jake's call (rev 3, 8/21 09:07 to 09:09).**
They carry the MLS Now watermark, so brand rule 7 kept them out of rev 2; Jake
reviewed the rendered rev 2 and asked for the before collage on slide 02 and a
before-to-after read on the room slides. Rev 3: slide 02 is the 4 x 2 collage
(watermark cropped, the person at the edge of unit1-bedroom excluded by crop)
under his "A vacant triplex in poor but inhabitable condition."; slides 03 to 07
are split frames, before left, after right, brass chips on each half. The
question "are the MLS photos his to publish" is now more important, not less,
and stays first on the confirm-with-Shavit list.

**Facts, not emotion.** Every adjective is his. The house is the villain
("vacant", "underperforming", "poor but inhabitable"); the street never is.

**Fair housing.** The one sentence about people is his L37, who did lease.

**Cadence.** Sunday 8/23 for the carousel. The Facebook post runs Monday 8/24
and leads on his community-impact line instead of the one-liner.
