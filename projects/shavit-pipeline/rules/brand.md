# Shavit Rootman — brand rules (prose; compiled from the Shavit Register, video-brain/Shavit.md)

These can't be regexed. Claude enforces them at draft time; the review page surfaces
them as a checklist. Machine-checkable siblings live in lint (no-dollar, full-states,
no-house-numbers, phone-registry, uncited-numeral).

1. **Reputation, not virality.** The audience is lenders, partners, sellers, tenants.
   Kill any hook that optimizes shares over standing. ([[reputation-not-virality]])
2. **Disparage the house, never the street.** A hook may criticize the property's
   condition; it may never knock the street, neighborhood, or community. Two drafted
   hooks died on exactly this line. ([[disparage-house-not-street]])
3. **De-identify people.** Anyone captured in a story or image is anonymized before
   publish; the bank-manager story runs as on-screen type under a generic tag.
4. **Full state names.** Michigan, Ohio, Indiana — always spelled out.
5. **No dollar figures anywhere.** Proof line instead: MADE IN AMERICA · LOCAL TO THE MIDWEST.
6. **Street names or project nicknames only** — never digit-leading addresses
   (protects tenants and vacant mid-rehab properties).
7. **No Zillow/portal imagery; no generated architecture.** Every pixel of a real
   property originates from a real photograph of it.
8. **Brand tokens:** black canvas, white type, brass rules, gold mark + one stake line,
   uppercase Inter. Logo draws once, then holds static.
9. **Cadence:** 2–3 posts/week ceiling, pinned brand film anchors the profile.
10. **Templated formats win.** Repeatable systems over one-off productions.
11. **Storytelling doctrine (Shavit call, 8/25/26).** Copy NEVER transcribes the photo —
    if the viewer can see it, the line is dead weight ("condensers outside... well,
    duh"). The photo carries the WHAT; the copy carries the WHY: the competitive
    advantage, stated market-specifically ("Most rental homes in Hillsdale do not
    include air conditioning"). Porsche rule: no comparisons, never "better than" —
    state the standard, never punch at competitors. Photo-transcription copy also makes
    every completed-project post read the same; advantage-led copy is the differentiator.
    Think brand strategist, not designer. First applied: Unit B deck rev 15, his 8/25
    12:44 PM rewrite (the reference register for future drafts).
