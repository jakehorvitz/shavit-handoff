3220 Clarendon Avenue
Cleveland, OhioA triplex transformed into three professionally managed homes through the BRRRR investment strategy.
Project Overview
Sometimes the best opportunities come from relationships rather than searching the market.While helping a homeowner find housing through one of our Charger Realty rental properties, Adam mentioned that if her pending home sale ever fell through, we would be interested in purchasing it. Nearly two months later, she reached back out after the transaction did not close, and we were able to put together a deal that worked for everyone.By the time we finalized the purchase, one of the three units had become vacant, allowing us to begin renovations immediately while planning improvements for the remainder of the property.

The BRRRR Strategy
Buy
This opportunity originated through a relationship rather than traditional marketing. When the seller's previous contract fell through, we were able to move quickly and acquire the property.
Rehab
Approximately $130,000 was invested to modernize the triplex, including new kitchens, new bathrooms, drywall repairs, updated plumbing and utility lines, cosmetic improvements throughout, and the installation of air conditioning in all three units.
Rent
Following completion, the property was fully leased with two three-bedroom, one-bath units and one one-bedroom, one-bath unit, producing $4,975 in monthly rental income.
Refinance
The completed property appraised for $550,000. Through refinancing, approximately $150,000 of capital was returned after closing costs, allowing us to continue investing in future projects.
Repeat
Rather than selling the asset, we retained ownership and redeployed returned capital into additional acquisitions.
