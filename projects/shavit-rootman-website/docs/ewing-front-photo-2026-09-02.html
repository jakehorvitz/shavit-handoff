Why the two men cannot simply be cropped out

This is geometry, not taste. The card frame is square with object-fit cover, so whatever is supplied gets centre cropped to 1:1.

Source frame: 2003 wide x 2402 tall
House spans roughly x 374 to 1869, about 1575px of width
The two figures begin at about y 975 and run to the bottom edge

A square that clears them must be at most 975 tall, so at most 975 wide
A square that holds the house must be at least 1575 wide
975 < 1575, so no square crop of this photograph holds the house and clears the people.
