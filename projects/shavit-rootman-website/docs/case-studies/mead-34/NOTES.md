34 Mead Street
Hillsdale, Michigan
A distressed home transformed into a professionally managed family rental through the BRRRR investment strategy.

Project Overview
Some of our best opportunities begin with simply letting people know we are willing to help.

After receiving one of our "We Buy Houses" mailers distributed throughout Hillsdale, the homeowner contacted us. She was experiencing significant financial hardship, the home had deteriorated over many years, and its condition prevented her from obtaining homeowners insurance.

Rather than seeing a property beyond repair, we saw an opportunity to solve a difficult situation while preserving another home for the Hillsdale community.

Following the purchase, we completed a comprehensive renovation focused on long-term ownership. Today, the property is professionally managed, fully occupied, and provides quality housing for a local family.

The BRRRR Strategy
Buy — This property was acquired through our direct mail marketing campaign. After receiving one of our "We Buy Houses" brochures, the homeowner contacted us seeking a solution for a property that had become difficult to maintain. Because we specialize in value-add investments, we were able to move quickly and purchase the home directly.

Rehab — Approximately $81,000 was invested to completely transform the property. The renovation included: Structural and deferred maintenance repairs / New drywall throughout / New flooring / Completely renovated kitchen / Updated bathroom / New lighting and fixtures / Interior and exterior paint / Updated mechanical systems / Landscaping and exterior improvements. Rather than applying cosmetic upgrades, the goal was to create a durable, low-maintenance home that would perform well for years to come.

Rent — Following completion, the property was professionally leased for $1,975 per month. Our goal is not simply to renovate homes—it is to provide housing that tenants are proud to call home.

Refinance — After stabilization, the property appraised for $287,000, creating approximately $148,500 in equity. The refinance allowed us to recover a significant portion of our invested capital while retaining ownership of the property and continuing to build long-term wealth through rental income.

Repeat — Capital returned through refinancing was reinvested into additional acquisitions, allowing us to continue expanding our portfolio while preserving quality housing throughout Hillsdale.

Lessons Learned
Every project teaches us something. For this investment, three lessons stood out:
Off-market opportunities often come through consistent outreach and strong community relationships.
Solving structural and mechanical issues before cosmetic improvements creates better long-term outcomes.
Standardizing materials and finishes improves efficiency while reducing future maintenance costs.

Community Impact
This project represents much more than a successful investment. The project:
Preserved a home that was at risk of continued deterioration.
