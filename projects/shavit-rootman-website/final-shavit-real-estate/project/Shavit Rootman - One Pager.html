Meet the Operator

Shavit Rootman was the first Israeli student to attend Hillsdale College, arriving in 2016. He chose Hillsdale over the Ivy League and financed his education through grants, scholarships, and merit-based aid.

Before America, he served in Duvdevan, an Israeli Defense Forces special operations unit, running counter-terrorism operations. The same operating discipline now runs the portfolio: trained teams, weekly execution sheets, accountability across every trade.

He is the founder of Charger Property Management: one operator, three housing classes, and a network of 10+ operating entities across Michigan, Ohio, and Indiana.

Three Housing Classes
  • Long-Term Residential — working families across core Midwest markets
  • Mid-Term Professional — furnished 30–180 day stays, Cleveland
  • Overlooked Assets — distressed homes, fully renovated
