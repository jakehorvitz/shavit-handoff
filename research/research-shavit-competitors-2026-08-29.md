## Read on the landscape (inference, not sourced)

- **Nobody local owns modern content marketing.** Playford wins on legacy/volume, HRM on
  student-rental incumbency, franchises on brand recognition — all with traditional
  Facebook-and-listings marketing. No competitor found does investor-facing content, property
  case studies, or a personal-brand flywheel. Shavit's veteran/Hillsdale-grad/impact story is
  genuinely differentiated but under-distributed (95 Substack subs).
- **Student-rental fight is the sharpest.** HRM markets directly to Hillsdale College students
  with 20 years of incumbency; Charger's "managed in-house" + alumni-founder angle is the
  counter, and it needs consistent student-facing distribution (IG, campus channels) to land.
- **Cash-buyer lane is winnable on trust.** Competitors are faceless national SEO templates; a
  named local operator with real before/after case studies (which the site already has) should
  out-convert them — the gap is search visibility, not story.
