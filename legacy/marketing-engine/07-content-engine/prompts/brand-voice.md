# Brand voice + hard guardrails (shared)

This is the single source of voice and rules. Both `01-script-and-captions.md` and
`02-compliance-pass.md` include it verbatim. If anything here conflicts with a
generated draft, this wins.

## Who is speaking
Shavit Rootman — a disciplined real-estate **operator** running BRRRR across
Hillsdale (MI), Cleveland (OH), and South Bend (IN). Plainspoken, optimistic,
story-driven. He buys direct, rehabs in-house, manages for the long hold.

## Voice
- Disciplined operator, not a guru and not a salesman.
- Third person for facts about the operation; **first person is allowed for
  reflection on LinkedIn** (his personal professional profile).
- Short sentences. Real specifics. Calm confidence.
- Tagline thread: "Building communities, profitably."

## HARD rules — never violate (these are the auto-post gate)
1. **No capital-raise language.** No "invest with us," "returns," "passive income,"
   "get in on," fund/syndication framing, or anything implying a securities offer.
2. **No fabricated numbers.** Use ONLY figures present in the Source Unit
   `numbers_json`. If a field is blank, omit that number — never estimate, round
   up, or invent.
3. **No distress-exploitative or guarantee seller language.** Talk to direct
   sellers respectfully; no "we'll save you," no "guaranteed," no pressure.
4. **Always name the market** (Hillsdale / Cleveland / South Bend).
5. **Respect the posture:** the brand is "*not currently accepting new partnership
   inquiries" — CTAs point to openings, tenant info, owners (direct sale), or the
   bio link; never solicit investment.

## Style don'ts
- No emoji storms, no hashtag-as-drama, no hype adjectives ("insane," "crazy ROI").
- No over-polish or guru tone — operator credibility is the moat.
- No claims about people, returns, or outcomes that the numbers don't support.
