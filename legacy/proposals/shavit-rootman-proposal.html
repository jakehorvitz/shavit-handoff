The Service

A content engine built around the formats that actually perform for property and rehab brands, the ones with the highest save and share rates.

Signature Brand Film

One cinematic, pinned introduction, the first thing anyone sees. "I get it" in sixty seconds, before a word is read.

Before / After Reel System

The single highest-save format in real estate. Templated split-screen and trending-transition reveals, gutted to finished, duplicable across every project.

Restoration Story Arcs

Multi-post project storytelling, from neglected to income-ready: strip-out → milestones → finished unit. How the strongest operator accounts build authority.

Progress Sequences & Reveals

Milestone photos stitched into satisfying progress reels, plus room-by-room finished reveals, a top-performing format, built entirely from stills.

ROI & "Behind the Build"

"Here's what it became" cost-and-craft reels that show repositioning with discipline, plus crew/testimonial trust content, the reputation drivers in construction.

Uniform colors mirroring the website, consistent watermark, clean typography, no pulsing, no gimmicks. Every piece reads the same way: quiet, premium, unmistakably yours.

The Operating Model

The goal is a consistent, tasteful presence, not virality. The only question is whether the system can keep it effortlessly fed. It can.

Step 01

Teams Snap Photos

Your crews are already on every site. They grab before / progress / after photos on their phones, no extra trips, no new hire.

Step 02

One Shared Folder

Photos land in a simple shared folder per project. I direct the shot list; the teams fill it.

Step 03

Batch Edit

One weekly block turns each photo set into 8–12 finished posts, before/after reveals, carousels, motion reels.

Step 04

Scheduled Drip

Posts released on cadence across IG, TikTok, YouTube, a steady stream, never a scramble.

Per-Project Photo Set

  • "Before" photos, every room + exterior
  • Progress snaps at each milestone
  • "After" photos, same angle as the before
  • 3–5 hero detail close-ups of the finish
  • Optional: any quick phone clip from the crew

Supply vs. Demand

Your content surplus, in one chart
The math: 7 active projects × 8–12 posts each = 56–84 posts banked, replenishing as projects advance. At a tasteful 2–3 reels/week you need only ~10–12 a month, so the presence stays effortlessly consistent and never forced. The point isn't volume or virality; it's that the brand simply never goes quiet again.

The Scorecard

You said you need measurables to judge me against. So I drafted them, a fair starting point for us to agree on together, then hold me to. Here's how we decide, honestly, whether I'm earning it.

  • Output & Reach25
  • Engagement & Quality25
  • Brand & Business Impact20
  • Consistency & Reliability20
  • Communication & Strategy10
CategoryHow it's scored (each criterion 1–5)
Output & Reach · 25Posting cadence hit vs. agreed calendar (≥90% shipped) · reach/impressions growth quarter-over-quarter · follower growth %.
Engagement & Quality · 25Engagement rate vs. baseline · saves / shares / comments (intent, not just likes) · top-post performance vs. prior period.
Brand & Business Impact · 20Inbound interest & DMs attributable to social · brand-lift (recall / favorability) · reputation signal among partners & investors.
Consistency & Reliability · 20Deadline adherence % · on-brand, error-free rate (revisions needed) · zero unexplained gaps in cadence.
Communication & Strategy · 10On-time reporting · proactive ideas/experiments proposed per period · responsiveness.

Cadence: a 15-minute monthly health-check (cadence + reach + engagement), a full quarterly score, and 6- and 12-month decision gates. Each level is anchored to a concrete number we set together at kickoff, so scores are evidence, not opinion.

The fairness rules, for both of us: these targets are set together at kickoff, not imposed.
