What I Need From You to Take the Marketing Live

A one-time handover. Once these are in place, the whole system runs on its own — you stay focused on the projects, and the brand never goes quiet.

How to read this: Items marked Blocks launch are the hard gates — nothing publishes until they're done. Everything else is refinement we can collect as we go. You don't need to gather it all at once; just the three on the critical path below get us live.

The Critical Path — these three get us live

  1. Meta Business Suite admin on Instagram + Facebook (so I can schedule and publish)
  2. Shared Drive folder + one crew-lead contact per project (the content engine)
  3. Brand kit — logo files + exact color codes (so everything is uniform and on-brand)
1
Access & Accounts
Admin access — never your passwords. Add me as a manager.
Blocks launch
  • Instagram — admin on the account via Meta Business Suite
  • TikTok — login or admin access
  • YouTube — channel access (Brand Account → add me as Manager)
  • Facebook Page + Meta Business Suite admin — this is what lets IG & FB schedule together
  • Google Drive — a shared folder for all project assets
  • Newsletter platform login (Beehiiv / Mailchimp), or the OK to create one
  • shavitrootman.com — registrar + hosting/DNS access, or your developer's contact
2
Brand Kit
So every post is uniform and mirrors the website — exactly as you asked.
On-brand
  • Logo files — vector (SVG/AI/EPS) + transparent PNG, all variants (full, mark-only, white)
  • Exact brand colors — hex codes for the navy + bright orange
  • Brand fonts — the typefaces used on the site (or where to license them)
  • Sub-brands — Charger Realty, DSR, Charger Property Mgmt, Baroutman: which get featured
  • Watermark — the exact mark + placement you want on reels
  • Existing collateral — any past materials you like the look of
3
Content Pipeline
The real engine. Your crews are the camera — I work from their photos.
Blocks launch
  • One crew-lead contact per active project + the OK to message them directly
  • Buy-in that crews shoot the photo set — before / progress / after, matched angles (5-min ask)
  • A shared photo drop per project — WhatsApp album or Drive folder
  • A starter backlog — existing before/after photos from current projects, so we launch with content banked
  • Approval to feature specific projects/addresses publicly (flag any that stay private)
  • Partners/people you want tagged or credited — and any you don't
4
Website
For the site build & maintenance deliverable.
Website
  • Current site content — copy, portfolio/property info, team bios, photos
  • What stays vs. changes — reskin, rebuild, or new pages?
  • Integrations — contact-form destination, CRM, listing feeds, analytics
  • Maintenance scope — confirm what ongoing upkeep covers
5
Sign-offs & Guardrails
Cheap to settle now, expensive to skip — these set the pace.
Align
  • Approval lane — do you review before publish, or trust me to ship + review monthly?
  • Voice do's & don'ts — anything off-limits (no figures? no client names?)
  • Cadence — confirm the 2–3 reels/week ceiling
  • Evaluation window — lock the 6-month–1-year scorecard period
