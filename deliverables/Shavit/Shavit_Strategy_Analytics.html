The operation is real

A track record, not a pitch.

Fifty doors across three states, an in-house rehab and management engine, and a company spun up per cycle for clean legal separation. The marketing job is to make that operation visible.

Where the doors are

Three states, one operator.

Hillsdale & Jackson, Michigan. Cleveland, Ohio. South Bend, Indiana. The story is geographic focus, not scatter — out-of-state investing done with boots on the ground.

  • Michigan — long-term & student/faculty housing; the home base.
  • Ohio — short / mid-term in Cleveland.
  • Indiana — newest expansion, South Bend.
The vision

50 doors today. 200 by 2030.

The growth curve is the narrative. Every rehab, every closing, every new job is a data point on the way to 200 doors, 100 homes revitalized in Hillsdale, and 25 local jobs.

The people who matter

Three audiences. One story.

Passive investors

Want returns without operating. We show them the machine works — consistent, documented, de-risked.

Aspiring investors

Want to learn the playbook. We teach in public — BRRRR, pre-foreclosure exits, in-house rehab.

Community & partners

Tenants, towns, equity partners. We prove we revitalize, not extract.

The method

Document the work.

The content strategy is not "post more." It's to turn the operation already happening into a steady feed of proof.

  • One rehab = a series. Before, during, after — the same property, multiple posts.
  • Local, useful, visible. Hillsdale-first content that the town actually cares about.
  • Operator voice. Plainspoken, confident, story-driven — never salesy.
  • Repurpose down-channel. Substack long-form → LinkedIn → Instagram cut-downs.
