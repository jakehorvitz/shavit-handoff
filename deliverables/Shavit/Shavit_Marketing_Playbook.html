The Thesis03 / 12
The Channel Call

The people who matter
aren't on LinkedIn.

  • Tenants in Hillsdale, Cleveland, and South Bend find homes on Instagram and Google — not a professional network.
  • Sellers — owners with a tired property or a deadline — respond to local trust and word of mouth, not feed posts for professionals.
  • Real estate is visual. A 20-second before/after says more than any text post ever will.
  • The movers and shakers in these markets are at city council, the closing table, and the jobsite. That's where we show up.

YouTube + Local07 / 12
The Engine

One rehab =
a month of content.

Capture

Phone clips on every site visit. 5 minutes, no setup. Walk the property, narrate what's broken and what's planned.

AI Assembly

Higgsfield animates stills into cinematic motion; auto-captions and cuts do the editing. ~30 min per reel, not 3 hours.

Publish

2 reels + 1 carousel weekly. Best clips stitch into the monthly YouTube debrief.

Route

Every caption ends with one tagged link or email — openings, owners, or the story.

Measure

Every click lands in the dashboard. Double down on what moves; kill what doesn't.

What good looks like.

Tenant Demand

Every opening has trackable traffic · 3–5 qualified inquiries per available unit · 70%+ of inquiries source-attributed.

90 Days11 / 12
Why This Strategy Wins

Built like the
portfolio: to compound.

  • It fits the operator. Content is documented from work already happening — sustainable for years, not a sprint that dies in week three.
  • It fits the audience. Tenants and sellers are local and visual. IG + YouTube + ground game reaches them where they actually are.
  • It fits the law. Nothing public solicits capital. Awareness compounds while partnerships stay private, one-on-one.
  • It's measured. One dashboard, monthly, with targets agreed up front. If a format doesn't move a number, it dies. No vanity metrics.
