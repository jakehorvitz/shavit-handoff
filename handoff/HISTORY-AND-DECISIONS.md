## Design and content decisions to preserve

1. **Use Shavit’s language and trace claims.** Clarendon’s audit tied published sentences to the supplied case-study document; retired facts and contradictory dates were explicitly listed. Keep captions, source files, fact IDs, coach notes, and lint evidence together. Never infer that a source-file fact has received the human registry approval.
2. **Carousel proof structure.** The August 21 standing pattern was: Shavit’s copy on the cover; before collage on slide 2; relevant room transformations shown together, before left and after right; close on exterior. The exterior can repeat where only one true exterior exists. Specific later client instructions take precedence, such as Howder’s interior-collage cover. Source: #8771, #9722.
3. **Maintain a consistent house style, respecting project-specific variants.** Product-edit reels specified black/white/gold `#FFC000`, uppercase Inter, static logo, real photography, and full state names. July branded cards used black canvas, SHAVIT ROOTMAN lockup, and brass-rule animation. Other carousel implementations used SF Pro/DM Sans substitutions. Keep actual templates and final renders; do not flatten all projects into one invented style guide. Sources: #981, #8020, #14233.
4. **Align type to the image and to its function.** Recent reel feedback emphasized flow, deliberate placement, words matching the pictured work, left-aligned bullet groups, centered headings, and ampersands instead of plus signs. Oak’s clean entrance image was selected because it fit the direct-access message and avoided debris. Source: September 6 Codex task.
5. **Address conventions matter.** Public case-study display titles omit house numbers. Full street identifiers still occur in source filenames and stable URL slugs. Holding-company names were removed from site copy. Unpublished information must also stay out of HTML/CSS comments. Sources: #8535, #8731, #12085, #13206.
6. **Geographic address and portfolio grouping differ.** Cedar/Niles is geographically Michigan, but Shavit groups it operationally under Indiana. The website uses separate `state: IN` and `addrState: MI` fields for that reason. Do not “correct” the operation grouping by changing the physical address. Source: #5605.
7. **Website CTA and Instagram identity are distinct.** `shavitrootman.com` is the primary rental/platform CTA. A search recorded the actual personal Instagram handle as `@shavitness`; older specs incorrectly used `@shavitrootman`. Verify the current account before new tags or publication. Sources: #9595, #9600.
8. **No automatic caption recycling.** In the Mead story work, Shavit rejected reused slide copy; the facts notes explicitly say shipped slide lines are not automatically reusable story captions. A brand line accepted for Howder was not automatically accepted for Mead. Sources: #10923, #10924.
9. **Crew features need real source material and name-specific permission.** The Clarendon crew skeleton prohibited substitute stock/generated crews and required permission per named person. Lewis approval did not carry over to other people. Sources: #8506, #8515.
10. **Cover provenance must travel with the design.** Mead rev16 records Jake selecting a supplied generated rendering as the cover after comparison with a real property photograph. The document explicitly requires identifying it as a rendering if sent onward or published. A real wide-frame alternative remained available in the renderer. Do not present that cover as documentary property photography. Later rev21 quality upgrades do not, by themselves, resolve or cancel this disclosure. Sources: #13043, #13148.
11. **Check exports, not just the editor.** Mead HQ work documented a Palmier ProRes/color problem and used H.264 4K followed by a controlled downscale to 1080×1920. Multiple final-looking filenames exist; select the latest verified export with its source timeline rather than assuming “FINAL” alone settles it. Sources: #11980, #12085.
12. **Check all relevant communication channels before a delivery.** September 6 correction explicitly broadened “scan Shavit’s texts” to the newest Shavit messages across personal and relevant group conversations, particularly CPM Onboarding. Feedback can arrive as annotated screenshots or video as well as plain text. Late August 31 carousel revisions demonstrated why this matters. Sources: #13055, #13494; September 6 Codex task.
